Built for movie night

  • Plays just about anything

    Movies and shows start right away, in nearly any format.

  • Cast to your TV

    Send what you're watching to your Chromecast or Google TV.

  • Gets to the good part

    Skip intros and recaps, then roll straight into the next episode.

  • Subtitles on demand

    Missing some? Search right from the player, with a subtitle plugin on your server.

  • Finds it fast

    Search, filter and sort until the perfect movie turns up.

  • Comes with you

    Download your favorites and watch them offline.

  • Watch together

    Press play with friends and stay perfectly in sync, wherever they are.

  • Your phone is the remote

    Pause, skip and change the volume from a Jellyfin app on your phone.
